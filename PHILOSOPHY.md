# FootLabOS Philosophy

## Mission

Maximize healthy days for people living with at-risk feet by making daily observation, human follow-up, and personalized manufacturing easier to sustain.

## Doctrine

### The person is the destination
FootLabOS exists to reduce friction for the person. The system should adapt to the client rather than demanding that the client become a software operator.

### Technology is the crew
Models, cameras, storage, automation, printers, and agents are tools. None of them becomes the authority over the person's body or care.

### We are not the doctor
FootLabOS does not diagnose disease, declare a foot safe, prescribe treatment, or replace podiatry, endocrinology, primary care, wound care, or other licensed professionals.

### Build the longitudinal truth
A single image is weak context. A consistent series of observations, paired with activity, footwear, insert version, client feedback, and review history, is materially more useful.

### Close the physical loop
The system should connect digital observation to a physical manufacturing response when appropriate: a revised insert, offloading geometry, material choice, fit change, or follow-up measurement.

### Local first, sharing by consent
The default architecture keeps client information on the assigned edge appliance. External access is an explicit product decision, not an accidental by-product of cloud software.
